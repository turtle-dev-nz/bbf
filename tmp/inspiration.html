Why It Matters

Brain cancer research is critically underfunded in Aotearoa

The registry is being developed with the New Zealand Aotearoa Neuro-Oncology Society (NANOS), an incorporated national society of over 80 clinicians, researchers, and support groups, linked internationally through the Asian Society of Neuro-Oncology.

"Brain cancer is the biggest cancer killer of children in New Zealand — 42% of all childhood cancer deaths."

Brain Tumour Support New Zealand

"The five-year survival rate for glioblastoma, the most common adult brain cancer, is just 5%."
