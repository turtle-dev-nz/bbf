The Story

Why Sam Runs

Sam has always believed that movement can change things — not just bodies, but minds, communities, and futures. That belief became personal when someone close to him was diagnosed with a neurological condition that turned their world upside down overnight.

The Big Brain Run is Sam's way of turning that grief into action.

Every dollar raised goes directly toward the Big Brain Foundation — supporting research, awareness, and the families who need it most.

No donation is too small. Whether you give $5 or $500, you're putting one more foot in front of the other alongside Sam. And together, we cover more ground than any of us could alone.
